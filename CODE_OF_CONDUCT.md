# Code of Conduct

Octopus is built for people who may be under financial stress. The project community should be respectful, practical, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but correct harmful ideas clearly.
- Use inclusive language.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Protect user privacy.
- Avoid shaming people for financial mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or abuse
- Discrimination
- Sharing private information without permission
- Promoting predatory financial products
- Encouraging unsafe financial behavior
- Intentionally misleading users or contributors

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block contributors who violate this code of conduct.
