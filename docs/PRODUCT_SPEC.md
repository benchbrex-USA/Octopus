# Octopus Product Specification

## Mission

Octopus helps households understand financial pressure early and choose safer next actions before debt, low savings, emergency expenses, inflation, or scams cause serious damage.

## Primary users

- Salaried workers with EMI-heavy cash flow
- Families with dependents and limited savings
- Gig workers with unstable income
- Young earners with low financial literacy
- Community organizations that want to self-host financial education tools

## Problem areas

### 1. High consumption debt and the EMI trap

Users often take small loans, BNPL plans, credit card rollovers, or multiple EMIs without seeing the combined monthly burden.

Octopus should show debt pressure clearly, detect when cash flow is becoming fragile, and suggest safer next actions such as freezing new borrowing, negotiating terms, prioritizing high-interest dues, and building a repayment plan.

### 2. Sharp drop in financial savings

Many users cannot save because the plan is unrealistic. Octopus should focus on starter buffers, small automatic transfers, sinking funds, and behavior-based savings plans.

### 3. Emergency expenses

A sudden expense can destroy cash flow. Octopus should help users estimate emergency readiness and identify gaps in savings, support networks, and coverage.

### 4. Stagnant income vs. rising living costs

Users need to see how rent, food, transport, school fees, utilities, and subscriptions change their financial flexibility. Octopus should provide cost-of-living simulations.

### 5. Low financial literacy and scams

Users need plain-language education and red-flag checks before making risky decisions. Octopus should explain offers, messages, promises, and contracts in simple terms.

## MVP scope

The MVP should include:

1. Household profile intake
2. Debt-pressure score
3. Monthly cash-flow squeeze score
4. Emergency buffer score
5. Scam-message red-flag checker
6. Plain-language action plan
7. Self-hosted deployment
8. Exportable report

## Non-goals

Octopus should not:

- Provide investment advice
- Sell loans or financial products
- Rank users for lenders
- Sell user data
- Hide recommendation logic
- Require AI for the core safety flow

## Success metrics

- Users can identify total debt pressure in under 5 minutes.
- Users receive a clear 30-day action plan.
- Recommendations show why they were generated.
- The product works without connecting bank accounts.
- The product can be self-hosted by a community group.

## Safety rules

- Never recommend taking more debt as the default answer.
- Never present estimates as guarantees.
- Always show assumptions.
- Always show a human-readable explanation.
- Always provide crisis-safe language for severe financial stress.
